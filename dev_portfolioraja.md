| Make it nice compatible with mobile, responsive | Audited the portal shell (`index.tsx`, `control-center.tsx`, `styles.css`) at 375px. This site was already far more mobile-aware than other Lovable scaffolds — `viewport-fit=cover`, safe-area padding, and most grids were already responsive. Found and fixed 3 real bugs instead of guessing: a hidden nav with no mobile replacement, a 600px decorative blur orb causing horizontal page scroll, and `min-h-screen`/`background-attachment:fixed` jank. Verified in-browser at 375×812 by measuring `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` and the orb's actual `getBoundingClientRect()` before/after. | **`hidden sm:flex` on a nav needs a mobile alternative, not just a hide.** The 3 anchor links (`#features`/`#how-it-works`/`#reviews`) simply vanished below `sm` with nothing replacing them — added a hamburger + dropdown panel, same minimal aesthetic as the rest of the site. |
| ↳ the real find | `#features` section was `relative` (no `overflow-hidden`) and contained a `w-[600px] left-1/2 -translate-x-1/2` blur orb | **Any absolutely-positioned decorative element centered wider than the viewport causes page-level horizontal scroll**, silently, because `body` overflow-x defaults to visible. Proved it in-browser: `orbRect.left = -112.5, orbRect.right = 487.5` against a 375px viewport — genuinely poking 112px past both edges — yet `document.documentElement.scrollWidth` stayed at 375 once `overflow-hidden` was added to the section. Every other section on this page already had `overflow-hidden`; this one was just missed. |
| ↳ verification method | Instead of trusting a screenshot, queried the DOM directly | `section.querySelector('.blur-3xl').getBoundingClientRect()` + `getComputedStyle(section).overflow` gave a definitive before/after, not a visual guess. Worth doing whenever a fix is "does X still overflow" rather than "does X look right." |
